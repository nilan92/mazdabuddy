* **Tag:** `SPEAKER 3 | 5:15 - 6:00`

* **Speaker 3 Notes:**
  > *"A fundamental managerial finance principle: buying spare parts is not an expense. It is an asset swap of cash for inventory. In small workshops, mechanics frequently buy parts with petty cash and forget to record them, leaving the books showing negative stock and phantom margins. AutoPulse locks down inventory integrity: every part is tracked with its cost price, selling price, and minimum reorder alert—stopping dead capital from gathering dust on shelves."*

## Managerial Finance Frameworks & Formulae Cheat Sheet

### 1. Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
$$\text{CCC} = \text{DIO} + \text{DSO} - \text{DPO}$$
* **DIO (Days Inventory Outstanding):** $\frac{\text{Average Inventory}}{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}} \times 365$  
  *(Compressed from 30 days to 12 days via JIT parts replenishment).*
* **DSO (Days Sales Outstanding):** $\frac{\text{Accounts Receivable}}{\text{Total Credit Sales}} \times 365$  
  *(Reduced from 20 days to 2 days via instant WhatsApp invoice & LankaQR payment).*
* **DPO (Days Payables Outstanding):** $\frac{\text{Accounts Payable}}{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}} \times 365$  
  *(Maintained at 15–20 days with transparent supplier credit tracking).*

### 2. Working Capital Requirement (WCR)
$$\text{Net Working Capital} = \text{Current Assets (Cash + Debtors + Stock)} - \text{Current Liabilities (Creditors)}$$
* AutoPulse unlocks **LKR 1.5M–2.0M** in idle capital by preventing unbilled Work-in-Progress (WIP) and dead stock accumulation.

### 3. Revenue & Margin Separation
$$\text{Total Invoice Revenue} = \text{Labour Revenue} + \text{Parts Revenue} + \text{Consumables} - \text{Discounts}$$
* **Labour Margin:** $\approx 82\%$ gross profit (pure technician billable time).
* **Parts Margin:** $\approx 28\%$ gross profit (pass-through reselling).
* *Managerial Rule:* Never discount high-margin labour to offset high-cost imported parts.

### 4. Accrual Accounting vs. Cash Accounting
* **Accrual Basis (AutoPulse):** Revenue is recognized when vehicle repair is completed; stock is credited and Cost of Sales debited simultaneously.
* **Cash Basis (Traditional):** Waiting until physical cash arrives distorts periodic performance and conceals outstanding debt.
