## What It Does

AutoPulse gives your workshop a digital backbone — from the moment a vehicle drives in to the moment the customer picks it up.

**For technicians:**
- See your assigned jobs at a glance
- Log parts used and labor time
- Get real-time updates as job status changes

**For managers & owners:**
- Full overview of every job on the floor — who's working on what
- Automatic invoices generated the moment a job is marked complete
- Low stock alerts before you run out of critical parts
- Financial overview: revenue, expenses, profit at a glance
- SMS customers automatically when work starts and when their vehicle is ready

**For customers:**
- Automatic SMS notifications — no more calls asking "is my car ready?"
- Professional PDF invoices

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Job Board** | Kanban view — drag jobs between Pending, In Progress, Waiting Parts, Completed |
| **Smart Scan** | Point your camera at a license plate — vehicle looked up instantly, no internet needed |
| **Auto SMS** | Customers notified when work starts and when vehicle is ready |
| **Invoicing** | Professional PDF invoices generated automatically on job completion |
| **Inventory** | Track parts stock, get low-stock alerts, auto-deduct when parts are used on jobs |
| **Finances** | Revenue vs expenses, profit tracking, expense logging |
| **Multi-Workshop** | Each workshop has its own isolated data, branding, and team |
| **Mobile First** | Designed for one-handed use on the shop floor |
| **Works Offline** | Job data cached locally — keeps working if WiFi drops |
| **Push Notifications** | Get notified on your phone when jobs are completed |

## Getting Started

The app is live at **[nilan92.github.io/mazdabuddy](https://nilan92.github.io/mazdabuddy/)**.

To install as an app on your phone (recommended):
1. Open the link in **Safari** on iPhone / Chrome on Android
2. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**
3. The app opens fullscreen with no browser bars

To register your workshop:
1. Open the app → tap **Initialize Workshop Portal**
2. Fill in your workshop name and details
3. Invite your team from **Settings → Staff & Access**

---

## For the Workshop Owner

- **SMS**: Set up text notifications in Settings → AI & Intelligence → SMS (uses [text.lk](https://app.text.lk))
- **Branding**: Upload your logo and pick your brand colour — it applies across the whole app
- **Team roles**: Admin, Manager, Technician, Accountant — each sees only what they need
- **Invoices**: Your shop name, logo, address, and terms appear on every PDF automatically
